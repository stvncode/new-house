The best filter for smart home purchases: **would you notice if it stopped working?** Automations you'd miss within a day are worth money; the rest is theater. Here's the house, room by room, through that filter.

## Whole-house baseline (before anything fun)

- **Interconnected smoke detectors** on every floor — smart ones alert your phone and can flash lights at night.
- **Leak sensors** under every water source: washing machine, dishwasher, water heater, under each sink. At ~€25 each, they're the best insurance-adjacent purchase in this hobby.
- **The hub**, wired to the network, on a small UPS.

*Baseline: €250–400.*

## Entrance & hallways

The highest-traffic automation in the house: **lights that turn on when you walk through and off when you leave**. Cheap PIR motion sensors are perfect here — transit zones don't need presence radar.

- Motion sensor + smart module or bulb per zone
- **Door sensor on the front door** (feeds "someone came home" logic)
- Optional: smart lock (keypad codes for family, temporary codes for cleaners), video doorbell

*Per zone: €60–90. Lock + doorbell add €350–450.*

## Living room

Where cheap motion sensors fail — you sit still, lights go off. This is **mmWave presence sensor** territory: they detect a person breathing on the sofa.

- Presence sensor + dimmable lighting (modules if you have neutral, bulbs otherwise)
- Motorized blinds/shutters — genuinely life-improving with sun-position automation
- A wireless scene button beats yelling at a voice assistant
- Temperature/humidity sensor feeding heating logic

*Range: €150 modest, €600+ with motorized blinds.*

## Kitchen

Less glamorous, more useful:

- **Leak sensor under the sink and dishwasher** (yes, again)
- Motion-activated under-cabinet lighting
- Smart plugs with power metering on kettle/coffee machine — doubles as "did I leave it on?"
- Contact sensor on the fridge if it's ever left ajar

*Range: €80–150.*

## Bedrooms

Restraint pays here. The wins:

- **Smart radiator valve or per-room climate** — schedule warm-before-wake, cool at night
- Bedside wireless button: one press = everything off
- Curtain motor if you like waking with light
- Skip presence-based lights in the sleeping area; nobody wants 3 a.m. surprises

*Per room: €80–250.*

## Bathroom

- Motion sensor with **humidity-triggered fan logic** — the automation everyone copies once they see it
- Leak sensor
- Heated towel rail on a schedule via smart plug or module

*Range: €70–120.*

## Office

- mmWave presence (you sit still here too) driving lights
- Power metering on the desk setup — reveals surprising standby waste

## Garage, laundry, technical

- Garage door: relay module + tilt sensor = open/close from anywhere, alert if left open
- Leak sensor by the water heater and washing machine
- Motion-activated light (you always enter with full hands)
- This is where your **energy meter (CT clamp)** lives, watching the whole house

*Range: €100–180.*

## What consistently disappoints

- Smart appliance integrations that require their own cloud app
- Color-changing bulbs everywhere (you'll use white 99% of the time — buy tunable-white)
- Anything whose primary interface is a phone app you must open — good automation removes interaction, it doesn't relocate it to a screen

Start with the baseline plus your three most-used rooms. Live with it a season. Your second wave of purchases will be twice as smart as your first.
